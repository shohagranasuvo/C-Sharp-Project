# Chapter 1: Introduction 

A diagnostic center plays a vital role in modern healthcare by providing patients with accurate medical testing and timely results. To ensure smooth operations, such centers need an efficient system to handle patient registration, staff management, test scheduling, billing, and record maintenance. Traditionally, these tasks were managed manually, which often led to delays, errors, and mismanagement of data. 

The **Diagnostic Center Management System** is developed to overcome these challenges by offering a digital solution that automates day-to-day operations. This system allows administrators to manage staff and resources effectively, receptionists to handle patient details and billing quickly, and patients to receive faster and more reliable services. It also keeps track of tests, accessories, and payment information, ensuring that all records are well-organized and easily accessible. 

By implementing this system, the diagnostic center can reduce paperwork, improve accuracy, and enhance the overall quality of service delivery. Patients benefit from quicker registration, transparent billing, and efficient test management, while staff members experience a smoother workflow and better coordination. Ultimately, this project aims to create a reliable, userfriendly, and comprehensive management system that supports the core functions of a diagnostic center. 

Chapter2: User Story 

## **Admin:** 

- **An Admin can perform Add, Update, and Delete operations on all system entities such as Tests, Accessories, Receptionists, Admin and also can see Billing information.** 

- **An Admin can manage user accounts (Receptionists and other Admins) with full CRUD control.** 

- **An Admin can update or modify the details of Tests and Accessories, including price, quantity, and disease information.** 

- **Admin holds the highest authority in the system and has unrestricted access to all features and data.** 

## **Receptionist:** 

- **A Receptionist comes next to the Admin.** 

- **A Receptionist can add, update, and manage Patient details, including registration, personal info, and contact details.** 

- **A Receptionist can generate bills for patients, including payment status, total amount, and remarks.** 

- **A Receptionist can update their own personal information.** 

- **Receptionists have limited access compared to Admins and cannot directly modify system-level entities like Accessories or Tests.**
